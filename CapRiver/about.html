Ethan A. Dunstan

Founder & Principal, Capital River Advisors

I founded Capital River Advisors after 25 years in commercial banking with UVA Community Credit Union, Virginia National Bank, and Wachovia Bank.

Over that career I built and presented loan requests, managed business client relationships, and took part in the credit discussions where financing decisions are made. Along the way I saw too many good businesses declined, or steered into the wrong kind of financing, simply because their story wasn't told the way a lender needed to hear it.

Capital River Advisors exists to close that gap. I bring a lender's-eye view to the owner's side of the table, and I lead every client engagement personally, working virtually with businesses wherever they're located.
